What This Platform Does

This platform compares DNA sequences against a reference FASTA sequence using sequence alignment and mutation analysis powered by Biopython.

It identifies single nucleotide variations (SNVs), codon changes, amino acid substitutions, and mutation positions.

Mutation locations are also visualized graphically across the sequence length.

Educational and research-use tool only. Not intended for medical diagnostics.
