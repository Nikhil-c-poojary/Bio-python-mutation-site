Scientific Interpretation

Amino acid analysis assumes that the supplied DNA sequence represents a coding sequence beginning at the first nucleotide. Insertions and deletions require additional reading-frame analysis.

Mutation effects are computational predictions based on sequence comparison. They do not establish clinical pathogenicity or disease association.
