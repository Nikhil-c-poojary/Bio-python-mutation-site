Scientific Interpretation

This tool identifies sequence variations and provides basic codon and amino acid interpretation. Translation assumes that the coding sequence begins at the first nucleotide and uses the standard genetic code.

Insertions and deletions may cause frameshifts. Their protein effects require additional reading-frame analysis. This educational tool does not determine whether a mutation is pathogenic or clinically significant.
